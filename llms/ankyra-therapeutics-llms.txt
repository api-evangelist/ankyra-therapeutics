# Ankyra Therapeutics

> Ankyra Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biotechnology company in Cambridge, Massachusetts developing "anchored" immunotherapies for cancer and immune-mediated diseases. Its platform tethers immune-modulating drugs to aluminum hydroxide so treatment concentrates at the disease site and minimizes systemic toxicity. Lead candidate ANK-101 is an anchored IL-12 therapy in clinical evaluation for advanced solid tumors. This is a company profile in the API Evangelist network; Ankyra publishes no developer API, SDK, or technical platform.
